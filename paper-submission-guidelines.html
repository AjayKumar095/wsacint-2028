Submission Guidelines

Authors are invited to contribute to WSACINT-2028 by submitting original research papers, technical studies, survey papers, project-based research and industrial case studies highlighting significant advancements in self-adaptive computational intelligence, neural networks and machine learning, fuzzy and evolutionary systems, networked and edge intelligence, autonomous technologies and emerging intelligent applications. Contributions addressing theoretical foundations, innovative methodologies, experimental developments, practical implementations and real-world applications are welcome. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following four technical tracks:

  • Track 1: Self-Adaptive Neural Networks, Deep Learning and Machine Learning
  • Track 2: Adaptive Fuzzy Systems, Evolutionary Computation and Swarm Intelligence
  • Track 3: Self-Adaptive Intelligence for Networked, Edge and Autonomous Systems
  • Track 4: Adaptive Computational Intelligence Applications and Emerging Directions

Interested authors are invited to submit full-length papers presenting original and previously unpublished research, including relevant results, figures, tables and references. All submissions must follow the prescribed IEEE conference paper format and applicable submission guidelines, and must not exceed six pages. Papers should clearly demonstrate technical contributions, research significance and relevance to the selected conference track.

Manuscripts must be submitted electronically through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT) using the official submission link provided below. Authors are required to ensure that their submissions comply with the prescribed formatting requirements, page limitations and submission policies. Each manuscript will undergo an appropriate peer-review process to evaluate its originality, technical quality, relevance and overall contribution to the conference.

Papers will be accepted only through electronic submission via Microsoft CMT using the official submission link provided below.

Plagiarism and AI-Generated Content Policy

All manuscripts submitted to WSACINT-2028 will undergo a double-blind peer-review process. Authors are therefore required to ensure that their identities, affiliations, acknowledgements, and other identifying information are not included in the initial manuscript submission.

WSACINT-2028 follows a strict policy against plagiarism and considers plagiarism and other forms of academic misconduct to be serious violations of research ethics. All submitted manuscripts will be screened for plagiarism and textual similarity. Manuscripts exceeding the permissible similarity threshold may be rejected without further review.

Only manuscripts with an initial similarity index below 15%, including references and DOI-related content, will be considered for further review. Authors are responsible for ensuring that their submissions are original and appropriately acknowledge all sources and prior work.

AI-Assisted and AI-Generated Content

Authors must ensure that the submitted manuscript represents their original scholarly work. The use of generative AI tools, including tools such as ChatGPT, to generate substantial portions of the manuscript, research content, analysis, results, or conclusions is not permitted unless explicitly allowed under the applicable IEEE policies and properly disclosed.

WSACINT-2028 reserves the right to conduct appropriate checks for plagiarism, manipulated content, and undisclosed AI-generated material at any stage of the publication process. If significant plagiarism, academic misconduct, or prohibited/undisclosed AI-generated content is identified at any stage, including after acceptance or registration, the manuscript may be rejected or withdrawn from publication, and registration fees, if already paid, may not be refundable.

Authors are solely responsible for the originality, accuracy, integrity, and ethical compliance of their submitted work.
