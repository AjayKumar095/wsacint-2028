Call for Papers

The 1st World Conference on Self-Adaptive Computational Intelligence and Networked Technologies (WSACINT-2028) invites original research contributions addressing the latest advancements in neural networks, deep learning, machine learning, fuzzy systems, evolutionary computation, swarm intelligence, continual and online learning, edge intelligence, IoT, intelligent networks, autonomous systems and emerging adaptive intelligent applications. The conference provides a world-level platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, practitioners and scholars to exchange knowledge, present innovative research, discuss emerging challenges and explore transformative solutions in self-adaptive, trustworthy, connected and autonomous intelligent technologies.

Authors are invited to submit full papers presenting original, previously unpublished research that is not currently under review or consideration by any other conference or journal. Submitted papers should be prepared using the IEEE double-column format and should not exceed six pages, including figures, tables, results and references. All accepted and presented papers will be considered for submission to the IEEE Conference Publication Program for possible inclusion in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library (subject to IEEE Computational Intelligence Society approval, applicable quality requirements and publication policies). All accepted papers must be presented at the conference to be considered for submission to IEEE Xplore. IEEE CIS technical co-sponsorship and publication in IEEE Xplore are subject to the necessary approvals; inclusion in IEEE Xplore is not guaranteed.

Conference Scope

WSACINT-2028 invites original, high-quality research contributions from researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, developers and practitioners worldwide. The conference aims to explore recent advancements, innovative methodologies, emerging technologies and real-world applications of computational intelligence that learns, adapts and optimizes itself across networked, edge and autonomous environments.

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Self-Adaptive Computational Intelligence and Autonomic Computing Systems
  • Neural Networks, Deep Learning and Intelligent Learning Systems
  • Online, Continual and Lifelong Learning, and Concept Drift Adaptation
  • Reinforcement Learning, Adaptive Control and Intelligent Decision-Making
  • Meta-Learning, Transfer Learning and Few-Shot Adaptation
  • Generative AI, Large Language Models and Foundation Models for Adaptive Systems
  • Spiking Neural Networks, Neuromorphic and Neuro-Symbolic Computing
  • Fuzzy Logic, Type-2 Fuzzy Systems and Approximate Reasoning
  • Adaptive Neuro-Fuzzy and Self-Tuning Fuzzy Systems
  • Evolutionary Computation, Genetic Programming and Memetic Algorithms
  • Swarm Intelligence and Bio-Inspired Computing
  • Dynamic, Multi-Objective and Constrained Optimization
  • Hybrid and Self-Configuring Computational Intelligence
  • Explainable, Trustworthy and Responsible Computational Intelligence
  • Robust and Resilient Learning, Adversarial Machine Learning and AI Security
  • Edge AI, TinyML and Energy-Efficient Learning on Constrained Devices
  • Federated, Distributed and Collaborative Learning
  • Computational Intelligence for Communication Networks, 5G/6G and Network Intelligence
  • Internet of Things (IoT), Industrial IoT and Intelligent Cyber-Physical Systems
  • Multi-Agent Systems, Agentic AI and Autonomous Decision-Making
  • Robotics, Autonomous Vehicles and Swarm Robotics
  • Digital Twins and Self-Optimizing Industrial Systems
  • Computer Vision, Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Sensing
  • Computational Intelligence in Healthcare, Bioinformatics and Biomedical Engineering
  • Computational Intelligence for Smart Cities, Energy, Climate and Sustainability
  • Smart Manufacturing, Intelligent Transportation and Industry 5.0

Within these thematic areas, authors are encouraged to submit research covering self-adaptive neural, fuzzy and evolutionary methods, continual and federated learning, trustworthy adaptation, edge and network intelligence, autonomous multi-agent systems, and CI-driven applications in healthcare, industrial environments, smart cities and sustainable infrastructure. The conference welcomes theoretical contributions, experimental studies, innovative methodologies and practical implementations addressing contemporary challenges in self-adaptive, connected and autonomous intelligent technologies.
