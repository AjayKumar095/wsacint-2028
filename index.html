About WSACINT-2028

Welcome to WSACINT-2028: The 1st World Conference on Self-Adaptive Computational Intelligence and Networked Technologies, organized by the School of Computer Science and Engineering on 21st – 22nd January, 2028 at Geeta University, Campus – Panipat, Haryana, India.

WSACINT-2028 is a world conference dedicated to promoting innovation, collaboration and knowledge exchange in self-adaptive computational intelligence and networked technologies. The conference provides a multidisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals and scholars to present their latest findings, explore emerging developments and discuss future directions in intelligent systems that learn, reason and adapt while they operate. In an era shaped by constantly changing data, connected devices, edge computing and autonomous machines, WSACINT-2028 addresses the growing need for computational intelligence that can adjust itself in real time, remain reliable under uncertainty, and perform efficiently across distributed and networked environments.

The conference encourages a synergistic integration of theoretical foundations, methodological advancements and practical applications, with a focus on impactful interdisciplinary research. The key areas include neural networks, deep learning, machine learning, reinforcement learning, continual and online learning, fuzzy systems, evolutionary computation, swarm intelligence, hybrid computational intelligence, edge intelligence, federated learning, Internet of Things (IoT), cyber-physical systems and autonomous multi-agent systems. These technologies have significant potential to transform sectors such as healthcare, smart cities, communication networks, transportation, agriculture, manufacturing, energy and sustainable development. WSACINT-2028 aims to strengthen academic excellence and international collaboration by connecting participants with researchers, technology experts and industry professionals, encouraging meaningful discussions and contributing to the advancement of next-generation self-adaptive intelligent technologies.

All accepted and presented papers will be submitted to the IEEE Conference Publication Program for possible inclusion in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library. (Subject to Approval)

Conference Objectives

WSACINT-2028 aims to promote high-quality research and facilitate the exchange of innovative ideas, emerging trends and technological advancements in self-adaptive computational intelligence and networked technologies. The conference seeks to establish a dynamic platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals and innovators to address contemporary challenges and explore future opportunities in intelligent, adaptive, connected and autonomous technological ecosystems.

  • Fostering interdisciplinary collaboration across neural networks, machine learning, fuzzy systems, evolutionary computation, swarm intelligence, edge intelligence, IoT and autonomous networked systems.
  • Promoting cutting-edge research and innovation in self-adaptive, continual-learning, self-tuning and self-optimizing computational intelligence methods that respond in real time to changing data, goals and environments.
  • Bridging theoretical research with real-world applications through practical solutions and innovative implementations in healthcare, smart cities, communication networks, intelligent transportation, agriculture, manufacturing and other application domains.
  • Encouraging knowledge exchange and international collaboration among researchers, academicians, industry experts, practitioners, doctoral scholars and young researchers, including members of the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society.
  • Exploring emerging challenges and future directions in continual learning, concept drift, explainable and trustworthy adaptation, federated and distributed learning, energy-efficient intelligence on edge devices, and resilient autonomous systems.
  • Facilitating industry–academia interaction to develop solution-oriented research addressing contemporary industrial, technological and societal requirements, while encouraging innovation, entrepreneurship and technology commercialization.

WSACINT-2028 aspires to serve as a world-class platform for exploring transformative research and emerging technologies in self-adaptive computational intelligence and networked systems. The conference will feature keynote addresses, technical paper presentations, interactive research sessions, workshops, tutorials and panel discussions led by distinguished researchers, academicians and industry experts. These activities will provide opportunities for participants to share knowledge, present innovative research outcomes, discuss technological challenges and identify potential directions for collaborative research and development.

The conference will bring together experts from diverse technical domains, including neural networks and deep learning, machine learning, fuzzy systems, evolutionary and swarm intelligence, edge and distributed intelligence, intelligent IoT and cyber-physical systems, and autonomous networked technologies. Through its four specialized technical tracks, WSACINT-2028 will encourage interdisciplinary research and facilitate discussions on integrating adaptive learning algorithms, intelligent optimization, networked infrastructures and autonomous technological solutions.

The conference further aims to support young researchers and students by providing opportunities to present their research, interact with experienced professionals, and develop academic and industrial connections. Through technical presentations, expert interactions and collaborative activities, participants will gain insights into emerging research methodologies, practical implementations and future technological possibilities.

We warmly invite researchers, academicians, scholars, innovators and industry professionals from across the globe to participate in this exciting forum and contribute to advancing innovation, academic excellence, knowledge exchange and international collaboration in self-adaptive computational intelligence and networked technologies.
